How accurate is it?

Fitted on 403 S&P 500 stocks from 2005 to 2019, then tested once on 422,747 forecasts from 2020 to March 2024, which it had never seen, and again on 2025 prices from a different source. On the test years its forecasts beat the industry-standard EWMA by 13% on QLIKE, the usual score for volatility forecasts, and the gap held in every year.

The dark band caught 71% of weeks where a perfect forecast would catch 68%, so it runs slightly wide. It forecasts the size of moves only: which way a stock goes next week is close to a coin flip, and nothing here claims otherwise.
